## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
CoinFlow is a simple expense tracker and budget planner that shows you where your money goes. Log spending in seconds, set monthly budgets, save toward goals and keep every account in one place.

TRACK EXPENSES IN SECONDS
• Add an expense or income with a few taps
• Pick a category, add a note, tags or a receipt photo
• Log past spending on any date
• Edit or delete any transaction, with undo

BUDGET PLANNER THAT KEEPS YOU ON TRACK
• Set a monthly budget for each category
• See how much is left and how much you can spend per day
• Get an alert before you go over budget

ALL YOUR MONEY IN ONE PLACE
• Separate wallets for cash, bank accounts, cards and savings
• 150+ currencies with live exchange rates
• Move money between wallets, even across currencies
• See your total balance in your main currency

SAVINGS GOALS
• Set a target and a deadline
• See how much to save each month to get there
• Add or withdraw money any time

BILLS AND INCOME ON REPEAT
• Rent, salary and subscriptions are logged for you: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly
• Pause or stop a repeating bill whenever you like

CLEAR MONTHLY REPORTS
• Income, spending and net for every month
• Spending by category at a glance
• Search and filter your full history
• Export to Excel for taxes or your own records

PRIVATE AND SECURE
• Your financial data is stored on your phone, not on our servers
• Lock the app with a PIN or your fingerprint
• Back up to Google Drive or any app you choose, and restore on a new phone
• No account and no sign-up needed

REMINDERS THAT HELP
• A daily reminder to log your spending, at the time you choose
• Notifications when a budget is nearly used up or a goal is reached

CoinFlow is free to use. Start tracking your expenses today and take control of your budget.

Questions or ideas? Email apputilitydroid@gmail.com
```
